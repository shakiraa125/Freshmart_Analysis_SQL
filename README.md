# FreshMart Retail Analytics – Branch Expansion Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

FreshMart Retail Analytics is a SQL-based business analysis project designed to evaluate retail performance and support data-driven branch expansion decisions.

The project analyzes sales, branches, employees, customers, products, suppliers, and inventory using PostgreSQL. SQL queries and analytical techniques were used to identify business trends, performance metrics, and areas requiring improvement.

## 🎯 Business Objective

FreshMart plans to expand by opening five new branches. The objective of this project is to analyze existing business data and provide insights into:

- Branch revenue and profitability
- Top-performing employees
- Customer purchasing behavior
- Top-selling and profitable products
- Category performance
- Supplier contribution
- Inventory issues
- Monthly sales trends
- Branch performance benchmarks for expansion

## 🗄️ Database Structure

The project uses a relational database containing the following tables:

- **Branches** – Branch information and locations
- **Employees** – Employee and branch details
- **Customers** – Customer information
- **Products** – Product, category, pricing, and supplier information
- **Suppliers** – Supplier details
- **Orders** – Customer orders and sales transactions
- **Order_Details** – Products and quantities included in each order
- **Inventory** – Product stock levels by branch

## 🔍 Analysis Performed

### 1. Branch Performance
- Calculated total revenue and profit by branch
- Calculated profit percentage
- Identified highest-revenue and highest-profit branches
- Compared branches against company averages
- Ranked branches based on revenue and profit

### 2. Employee Performance
- Calculated sales generated by each employee
- Identified top-performing employees by branch
- Compared employee sales with branch averages
- Ranked employees within branches
- Identified employees with no handled orders

### 3. Customer Analysis
- Identified repeat and one-time customers
- Calculated total customer spending
- Identified top customers
- Calculated Average Order Value (AOV)
- Identified customers purchasing from multiple categories
- Identified customers who never placed an order

### 4. Product & Category Analysis
- Calculated product revenue and profit
- Identified top-selling products
- Identified top products within each category
- Identified never-sold and slow-moving products
- Ranked products by revenue
- Calculated category revenue and profitability

### 5. Supplier Analysis
- Calculated revenue generated from supplier products
- Identified suppliers with never-sold products
- Identified suppliers supplying multiple categories

### 6. Inventory Analysis
- Identified out-of-stock products
- Identified products below reorder levels
- Identified products that were never ordered
- Calculated inventory value by branch

### 7. Sales Trend Analysis
- Analyzed monthly sales and profit
- Calculated running revenue
- Compared current and previous month sales
- Calculated Month-over-Month (MoM) growth
- Identified the best-performing sales month

- Branches → Employees
- Employees → Orders
- Customers → Orders
- Orders → Order_Details
- Products → Order_Details
- Suppliers → Products
- Products → Inventory
- Branches → Inventory
